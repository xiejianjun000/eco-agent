# eco Agent Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

eco Agent is an open-source AI agent product. To keep the ecosystem clear and avoid confusion, we offer these simple guidelines:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "eco Agent" to accurately describe your relationship with it, e.g. "built on eco Agent" or "compatible with eco Agent".
- When naming your own project, you are welcome to build on eco Agent, but avoid implying an official affiliation (e.g. do not use "eco Agent" as your own product name or logo).
- Use the official brand assets — the eco mark in ECO green `#0F766E` with a `#96D562` accent — when referencing the project.
- Do not modify, recolor outside the provided palette, or redistribute the logo in a way that misrepresents the project.

We appreciate the community's help in keeping attribution clear. Thank you for building with eco Agent.
